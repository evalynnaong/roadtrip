Stop 1 · Santa Barbara, CA

Where the
road began.

I had the pleasure of doing my undergrad at UC Santa Barbara where I surfed between classes and learned how to research. I worked as a Research Assistant for Professor Mattanjah de Vries and later as a full-time Research Chemist at Reaction35 LLC. There my professors and mentors gave me the permission to be inquisitive and supported my knack for engineering creative solutions.

2015-2021 UC Santa Barbara, Reaction35 LLC Chemist
2016-2021 · Santa Barbara, CA
Stop 2 · Bay Area, CA

Picking up
speed.

Chemistry took me back home to the Bay Area, where I worked as an Analytical Chemist for Balazs Nanoanalysis (Air Liquide). I got to work in close quarters with a great team and experience first hand a corporate version of the chemical industry.

Chemist Air Liquide Analytical Chemistry / Quality Control
2021-2024 · Bay Area, CA
Stop 3 · Bay Area, CA

The unexpected
detour.

Unlike working in academia or even at a startup, much of my work as a corporate chemist was bogged down in error reporting and task communication. Frustrated, I engineered my own solutions. I started with a simple error reporting form that we could scan via QR code in the lab. I was taking coding courses at night through Foothill college at the time and I wrote a script that would trigger an automated email report to send to our customer service reps. What used to be a lengthy (and sometimes forgotten) process turned into a 30 second task. From there, I was hooked. I coded numerous automation projects aimed at systematic documentation before leaving my role as a Chemist in spring 2024 and returning to school.

2024 · Bay Area, CA
Stop 4 · Bay Area, CA

Cruising at
altitude.

At this point I knew the direction I wanted to go, but my path was unclear and my car wasn't packed (My existing transcripts were disconnected to the field I wanted to pursue). The next two years were a thrill. To build up my knowledge base, I continued taking classes at Foothill College and enrolled in NYU's Tandon Bridge Bootcamp, a program aimed at prepping prospective graduate students for their school of engineering. As graduate applications opened, I started the beginning of a Post-bacc at CSU East Bay while applying to San Jose State University and UC Berkeley for my master's. I began my master's at San Jose State Spring 2025 and received my acceptance to UC Berkeley in the middle of the semester. It was a long a windy road, but I created a curriculum for myself that took me through all the necessary foundations to pursue my Master's in Statistics at UC Berkeley.

Lifelong Student Foothill College - CSU East Bay - San Jose State - UC Berkeley Master's in Statistics
